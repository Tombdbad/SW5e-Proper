# The starship kit: sizes, faction kits and references (2026-11-09)

Links only: no image is copied into the repository or the game (the pictures belong to Lucasfilm/Disney and their authors). The reference pictures sit on a Mixar moodboard (the scene tab "SW5e starship kit", seven frames, one per faction, each card with its source written under it); they were downloaded to a scratch folder outside the repo for that board only. The ships are original shapes built after the faction's design language, not copies of any one ship.

Built by `tools/blender/build-ships.py` (run headless in Mixar or Blender 5.2: `mixar.exe --background --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 --python tools/blender/build-ships.py -- client/public/figures [--lo] [--kit <scratch dir>]`, once without and once with `--lo`). It writes `client/public/figures/ships.glb` (44 meshes `ship_<faction>_<size>[_<role>]`, about 109,000 triangles, 5.6 MB) or, with `--lo`, `ships.lo.glb` for phones (rounds of fewer sides, half the dressing: about 55,000 triangles, 2.9 MB; its shapes are never larger than the full build's and at most about 6 per cent smaller), `shared/art/shipKit.json` (each ship's faction, size, role and box in metres) and, with `--kit`, `ships_kit.glb`: the 39 kit parts on their own for review in Mixar (not used by the game). Regions per vertex: 0 hull, 1 panel, 2 engine glow, 3 glass, 4 livery, 5 gunmetal; the game paints them per faction (`models.tsx` `SHIP_PAINT`). Faces meeting at under 40 degrees (62 in the phone's file) are shaded smooth, harder edges stay sharp.

**The dressing of the big ships** (second pass, 2026-11-09): the Large, Huge and Gargantuan ships carry decals laid on the hull by rays (`Hull.sticks`: a flat quad of two triangles where a ray first meets the hull, kept only where all four corners lie on the surface): rows of lit and dark ports (`ports`, `facade`), plating on a grid (`plates`), panel lines across and along the decks (`lines`, `spine_lines`), hangar mouths in a lit rim (`bay`); and parts stood on the hull's top: turrets (`guns`), masts with lamps (`mast`), shield domes (`domes`), blocks of superstructure (`blocks`). The dagger hulls share `dagger_dress`, the Mon Calamari ones `mon_cal_dress`. The Large ships, seen at their real size in a hangar, have 1,600 to 3,900 triangles; the Huge and Gargantuan 2,300 to 9,800.

## Sizes

The book (rulesStarships, "Size Categories") gives each size the space it controls, not its length; the kit keeps every ship's length inside its size's band. The map's scale is the people's: 0.711 squares to the metre (`models.tsx` `UNITS_PER_M`, a person of 1.70 m stands 1.21).

| Size | Book band | Kit ships (length) | Berth (squares, `SHIP_FOOTPRINT`) | Hangar storeys (`SHIP_STOREYS`) | Roles (ships.json) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tiny | up to 25 ft (7.6 m) | 2.6 to 4.7 m: drones, droid fighters | 5 x 4 | 1 | Droid, Munition, Predator, Probe, Satellite, Worker |
| Small | 25 to 50 ft (7.6 to 15 m) | 5.5 to 14.8 m: fighters, interceptors, shuttles | 11 x 9 | 2 | Attack/Superiority Fighter, Bomber, Scout, Scrambler, Shuttle |
| Medium | 50 to 250 ft (15 to 76 m) | 19 to 38 m: freighters, gunships, yachts | 28 x 20 | 3 | Courier, Freighter, Gunship, Missile Boat, Navigator, Yacht |
| Large | 250 to 2,500 ft (76 to 760 m) | 90 to 108 m: corvettes, light cruisers | 78 x 32 | 7 | Ambassador, Corvette, Cruiser, Explorer, Picket Ship, Ship's Tender |
| Huge | 2,500 to 10,000 ft (760 to 3,050 m) | 876 to 1,245 m: star destroyers, Mon Cal cruisers | stays in orbit | - | Battleship, Carrier, Colonizer, Command Ship, Interdictor, Juggernaut |
| Gargantuan | 10,000 ft and more (3,050 m +) | 3,060 to 3,470 m: dreadnoughts, a core ship, a mobile city | stays in orbit | - | Blockade Ship, Flag Ship, Industrial Center, Mobile Metropolis, Researcher, Warship |

The TIE-like fighter (5.5 m) is under the Small band's foot: the band is the space a ship controls, and the line fighter is the book's own Small example. Huge and Gargantuan ships never put down (the user, 2026-11-09): the helm offers them no landing (`shipLands`, house rule shown at the helm). On the starship-combat grid a ship takes the book's variant "Sizes on a Grid" (Tiny and Small 1 square, Medium 2, Large 4, Huge 8, Gargantuan 16) and is drawn at real size where that is smaller.

## Which kit a ship takes

By its owner (`models.tsx` `shipFaction`): Separatist, Trade Federation, Techno Union and the like -> separatist; Mandalorian, Death Watch -> mandalorian; Empire, Imperial, First Order, Sith -> imperial; Rebel, Resistance, New Republic -> rebel; Republic, Jedi -> republic; Hutt, Black Sun, pirates, Crimson Dawn, Pyke and cartels -> criminal; anyone else (a person, a guild) -> civilian. A Shuttle or Transport role takes the faction's shuttle where it has one (imperial, republic).

## Berths for a Large ship
A hangar keeps its visitors' berths by the code tag `berths:<n>[:<size>]` (Medium unless a size is given; `visitorBerthSizes`). A town's landing field keeps one for a Large ship (`berths:1:Large`: a 78 x 32 berth, the field seven storeys). (The freight hangar of a city spaceport was given one too, and dropped again: the bigger hangar shifted the random numbers of the world, and seed 2 then hit a planner flaw that is older than this change: a round three-floor room, its corridor too short to reach it, left cut off. See "Not done / next".) A party's ship takes the smallest free berth it fits, in the hangar it put down in or else anywhere in the place (`withPartyShips`); only where none is big enough does it take the largest free one, where it is drawn smaller to fit.

The round landing bay of a building's model (`BuildingModel`, the token of a spaceport, landing field or docking building before it is planned) holds the kit's civilian freighter fitted to its pit (a token, not drawn to the street's scale).

## Not done / next
- The server sends the files uncompressed: ships.glb is 5.6 MB (1.25 MB gzipped), the phone's 2.9 MB. Compression of the static files (or Draco/meshopt in the glTF) would cut the download.
- A station's docking arm keeps no visitors' berth: a party ship that docks there is placed only where a berth is free.
- The planner flaw above (`planBuildingRooms`, `joinStranded`): when a round room with a column of floors cannot be set against its corridor's far end, it is placed elsewhere and the rescue pass has nothing for it (no shared wall, no corridor of up to 9 squares). tests/world/layouts.test.ts seed 2 shows it when the freight hangar keeps a Large berth; a fix would try the room's other neighbours or an L-shaped corridor, then give the city freight hangar its Large berth back.
