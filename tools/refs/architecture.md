# Star Wars architecture: references by planet (2026-11-05)

Links only (no images are copied into the repository: they belong to Lucasfilm/Disney and their authors). Gathered by web search for the building and room overhaul; each entry says what the design is, so a model can be built from the description. **Gaps** are places the search found nothing usable; do not invent links for them.

The game's own worlds are generated by biome (`desert`, `ice`, `forest`, `jungle`, `swamp`, `ocean`, `volcanic`, `urban`, `temperate`, `barren`, `gas giant`, stations, ships), so each planet below says which biome it informs.

## Desert (Tatooine, Jedha, Geonosis, Mandalore's cube-cities)
**Tatooine** (Mos Eisley, Mos Espa, Lars homestead, Jabba's palace)
- Rounded, sunken adobe; whitewashed duracrete domes over low cubes; low profile against the heat. Filmed on Djerba and in Tunisia: the Mos Espa set copied local Tunisian building (plaster over frames).
- Lars homestead: mostly underground, a sunken courtyard (the "pit") with a central entry dome, moisture vaporators, dew-collecting jugs, a garage; on the Great Chott salt flat.
- Sources: [Mos Eisley, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mos_Eisley); [Lars Moisture Farm, Databank](https://starwars.com/databank/lars-moisture-farm); [Lars homestead, Wookieepedia](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lars_homestead/Legends); [Mos Espa City Hall](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Mos_Espa_City_Hall); [Entry dome](https://starwars.archivum.wiki/article/entry_dome); [Prequel location stills, Propstore](https://propstore.com/?p=3073); [Mos Eisley, 360 panorama](https://www.360cities.net/image/mos-eisley-starwars-city-on-desert-tunisia); [Ralph McQuarrie's cities](https://persquaremile.com/2012/03/05/star-wars-visionary-ralph-mcquarrie-reimagined-cities-too/); [The Book of Boba Fett concept art](https://www.brickfanatics.com/new-concept-art-for-star-wars-book-boba-fett); [Star Wars Battlefront II concept art](https://ea.com/en-us/games/starwars/battlefront/news/concept-art-of-star-wars-battlefront-ii)
- Gap: Jabba's palace has no source found.

**Jedha** (Jedha City)
- "Box with dome" Ottoman-era look; the Laleili Mosque is cited as a fit. Source: [Architecture of Star Wars, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture_of_Star_Wars); [The Architecture of Rogue One, Architizer](https://architizer.com/blog/?p=12182).

**Geonosis** (Petranaki Arena, hive towers)
- Geonosian hives: tall, tapering, spined spires of a termite-mound kind in red rock; the arena a great open ring with a main gate and a CIS overlook. Sources: [Geonosis, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geonosis); [Ryan Church arena concept (Battlefront II concept art page)](https://ea.com/en-us/games/starwars/battlefront/news/concept-art-of-star-wars-battlefront-ii).

**Mandalore**
- Self-contained bio-cube cities in the desert; cubic imagery with Cubist and THX 1138 influences. Source: [Fandom, 8 Fascinating Star Wars Planets](https://www.fandom.com/articles/8-fascinating-star-wars-planets) and [Unwinnable, The Architecture of a Generic Star Wars Planet](https://unwinnable.com/2017/07/17/the-architecture-of-a-generic-star-wars-planet/).

## Temperate and urban (Naboo, Coruscant, Alderaan, Corellia)
**Naboo** (Theed, the Royal Palace, Gungan cities)
- Theed: Italian, Baroque and Turkish mosque crossbreed; the palace on a cliff edge with domes and minaret-like towers (Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque), arcades and waterfalls, Art Nouveau and organic metalwork (Guimard, Horta, Wright). Interiors shot at Caserta Palace (Italy): vaulted halls, colonnades. Otoh Gunga: bubble-city, Art Nouveau metal in the manner of Jules Verne.
- Sources: [CNN, the architecture that inspired a galaxy](https://edition.cnn.com/style/article/star-wars-architecture-one-square-meter); [Domus, the architecture of Star Wars and Ralph McQuarrie](https://www.domusweb.it/en/architecture/2021/04/21/the-architecture-of-star-wars.html); [Style Magazine, Building an Empire](https://stylemagazine.com/news/2017/dec/12/building-empire-exploring-architecture-star-wars); [Theed, Wookieepedia mirror](https://breezewiki.discard.no/starwars/wiki/Theed); [ArchDaily, 7 iconic structures](https://www.archdaily.com/786751/the-architecture-of-star-wars-7-iconic-structures/); [Art-Critique, a closer look](https://www.art-critique.com/en/2020/05/may-the-fourth-be-with-you-a-closer-look-at-the-architecture-of-star-wars/); [Gungan sacred place concept art](https://intranet2.carder.gov.co/blog/starwarsconceptart/615631397530550272-the-gungan-sacred-place-was-a-ancient-construction)

**Coruscant**
- 1930s New York "on steroids": Art Deco and Art Moderne skyscrapers (Empire State, Chrysler), stepped setbacks, pyramids and pseudo-Gothic forms (Prambanan, Milan Cathedral for the Emperor's palace); the Senate a great dome (modernist, Brazil's Congress cited). Sources: [Style Magazine](https://stylemagazine.com/news/2017/dec/12/building-empire-exploring-architecture-star-wars); [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/style/article/star-wars-architecture-one-square-meter); [Gizmodo, how Star Wars changed architecture](https://gizmodo.com/how-star-wars-changed-architecture-5293034); [Fast Company, best architecture in the galaxy](https://www.fastcompany.com/1295882/best-architecture-star-wars-galaxy).
- Gaps: no Alderaan, Corellia or Coronet sources found. Corellia: [shipyard concept art, Ahsoka](https://intranet2.carder.gov.co/blog/starwarsconceptart/731816986301071360-concept-art-for-the-new-republic-shipyard-on) is the only hit.

## Ice and cold (Hoth, Ahch-To)
**Hoth** (Echo Base)
- Built underground in the ice: bored tunnels with conduits along one side, walls angled inward to carry the load, few finished rooms. Source: [moss-design, architecture of Star Wars](https://moss-design.com/architecture-of-star-wars/); [Architizer cross-sections](https://architizer.com/blog/inspiration/collections/incredible-cross-sections-star-wars/amp).
**Ahch-To**: beehive stone huts (box-and-dome family). Source: [Unwinnable](https://unwinnable.com/2017/07/17/the-architecture-of-a-generic-star-wars-planet/).

## Forest and jungle (Endor, Kashyyyk)
**Endor**: the Ewok village, huts and walkways in trees from local materials. Sources: [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/1295882/best-architecture-star-wars-galaxy); [Vernacular in space architecture](https://tboake.com/gravity/maemura/vernacular.htm).
**Kashyyyk**: Wookiee cities more streamlined and advanced than Ewok huts (same source). Gap: nothing found on Kachirho.

## Ocean and storm (Kamino)
**Kamino** (Tipoca City): the city on pillars over the sea, curved forms to shed wind and waves, landing platforms and a clone facility. Sources: [Arc Troopers concept art gallery, starwars.com](https://starwars.com/tv-shows/clone-wars/arc-troopers-concept-art-gallery); [starwars.com series gallery](https://starwars.com/series/clone-wars/arc-troopers-concept-art-gallery).

## Gas giant and station (Bespin, Death Star, Scarif)
**Bespin** (Cloud City): a floating mining city, saucer and smooth forms because it must float; roots in Laputa; McQuarrie's first sketches an Imperial base. Sources: [moss-design](https://moss-design.com/architecture-of-star-wars/); [Unwinnable](https://unwinnable.com/?p=64352); [CBR](https://www.cbr.com/star-wars-cloud-city-basically-original-death-star/); [nwahomepage](https://digital-release.nwahomepage.com/?p=151214).
**Scarif**: incongruous industrial architecture of startling scale; ship-refit centre; ring-shaped early concepts. Sources: [Architizer, Rogue One](https://architizer.com/blog/?p=12182); [Doug Chiang on imperial architecture, starwars.com](https://starwars.com/news/designing-an-empire-doug-chiang-on-imperial-architecture-in-rogue-one).
